## Suggested excerpt
RustChain's Proof-of-Antiquity flips the usual mining story: raw speed is not the only thing that matters. The network tries to bind participation to a distinct physical CPU, then applies time-decaying bonuses to vintage hardware. This field note looks at the fingerprinting and reward logic without treating the multipliers as a promise of profit.
